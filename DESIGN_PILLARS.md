# Design Pillars

## Oppressive Bureaucracy
Gameplay should feel procedural, repetitive, and systemic, like working through forms and service flows in a failing state office.

## Uncertainty
Player should never receive perfect information; cases are solved through inference, partial clues, and unreliable testimonies.

## Ritual / Sanation Tension
Ritual / Sanation should feel dangerous, clinical, and strict — a procedure with consequences, not magical spectacle.

## Post-Soviet Mundanity
The occult emerges from ordinary bureaucracy, broken infrastructure, old instructions, and tired people.

## No Power Fantasy
Player is a clerk-investigator, not a hero; success is measured by correct diagnosis and completed procedure.
